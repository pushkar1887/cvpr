## The method (final row, equal budget)

`route:gate_jitter_merge_sched_pa|gate_jitter_merge_pa_eq` — training-free, no attention scores:

1. Foreground gate: tokens whose foreground fraction is below 0.5 are dropped and discarded.
2. Coverage: one random token per Hilbert-curve stretch of the foreground (k stretches).
3. Merge: each dropped foreground token is averaged into its most cosine-similar kept token.
4. Size-aware attention: + log s on each kept token's logit in every LLM layer (s = tokens merged into it).
5. Routed schedule: one-token answers (option letter, yes/no) keep 20.3% / 18.4% of N until layer 20 / 26 and 6.8% /
   6.1% after; generated answers keep a flat 16.4% / 15.0% (Lingshu / Qwen3-VL). Same mean tokens per layer as VScan.

At 10% of tokens kept (the pre-LLM budget) the same pipeline runs without step 5.
